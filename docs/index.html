Engine Features

Built for Serious Worldbuilders

From antique Tolkien-inspired fantasy parchments to satellite bathymetry and 3D terrain heightmaps, Axis Map Generator delivers instant cartographic results.

🌍

Multi-Octave Fractal Worlds

Procedural continents, archipelagos, pangaeas, shattered isles, and caldera ring atolls generated with customizable sea level, roughness, and mountain ridging.

🏔️

Miniature Tolkien Mountain Ridges

Isometric mountain peaks with sunlit slopes, hand-engraved shadow hatching, snowcaps, and foothill hummocks clustered naturally along continental ridgelines.

💧

Hydraulic Flow Simulation

Real moisture and hydraulic flow accumulation carving dendritic river networks, tributaries, inland lakes, and estuaries out to concentric ocean wave ripples.

👑

Multi-Realm Political Dynasties

Procedural heraldic capitals with citadel keeps, pennant flags, castles, and town markers. Click any label on the canvas to rename or drag to reposition.

🖌️

Live Manual Terrain Sculpting

Interactive Raise, Lower, and Smooth brushes with configurable brush radius and intensity. Sculpt custom mountain ranges or carve bays in real time.

📦

3D Heightmap & Vector SVG Export

Export crisp 4K/8K PNGs, scalable vector SVGs, and 16-bit Grayscale Heightmap PNGs ready for immediate displacement in Blender, Unreal Engine 5, and Unity.
